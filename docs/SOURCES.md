## Notes

- **Two roll layers.** `bcpa-taxroll` (Broward GIS, latest sale 2024-09-27) is the primary roll.
  `bcpa-taxroll-full` (older snapshot, latest sale 2020-03-26) adds the 233,551 folios the primary
  layer lacks — chiefly 212k condominium units. Those parcels carry the older layer's vintage
  and provenance (`roll_source = 'full'` in `v_property`).
- **Permit → parcel linking.** The Fort Lauderdale layer leaves `PARCELID` empty on 61 % of rows.
  Permits without a folio link by street address when the normalized address (+ unit) names
  exactly one parcel in the permit's city; ambiguous addresses stay unmatched. This run: folio
  35,765, address 37,191, address + unit 8,409, unmatched 9,570 (89.2 % linked, QA `warn`).
  Unmatched rows are mainly placeholder addresses (`1 ALARM LABEL`, `999 UTILITY ST`) and
  multi-parcel buildings named without a unit. `sp_permit_details.parcel_match_method` records
  the method per permit.
- **Contractors.** The FTL layer's `CONTRACTOR` column is empty, so the licensed professional comes
  from the bounded Accela CapDetail lookup (1,598 open roofing permits, 1 h 37 min at 1.5 s per
  request, no blocks). 551 records show a license number; 476 resolve to a DBPR license.
- **Permit coverage** is 2 of 32 jurisdictions (Fort Lauderdale, Weston; 14 % of parcels). The
  rest are blocked (HCED query failure, captcha/login portals) or skipped as parcel-by-parcel
  portal searches (§3.7); roof age there falls back to the year built.
